Gatley's the shop. The catchment is everywhere worth driving from.

BXRBERS is on Silverdale Road in Gatley, SK8 4RF. Most of our regulars drive in from neighbouring towns — and "drive in" usually means three to fifteen minutes, not half an hour. Free parking on site, online booking always live, walk-ins welcome.

Not On The List?

We also cut for clients from Wilmslow, Hale, Altrincham, Sale, Northenden, Wythenshawe, Hazel Grove, Poynton, and across Greater Manchester. If you can drive to Gatley, the chair's there. Free parking outside means no faffing when you arrive.

Coming from further out? Book in advance on Booksy and pair it with something else on the high street — Gatley has a few decent places to eat and a good independent coffee scene. Make a thing of it.
