Barber for
Stockport.

Ten minutes from the Underbanks. Worth every mile.

Stockport's barber scene has come a long way in the last five years. Plenty of decent shops. But "decent" isn't what we built BXRBERS for. The Stockport clients who find us are the ones who've tried everywhere else and want the cut taken seriously. Ten minutes by car. £20 a cut.

The Journey

From Stockport town centre (SK1) it's about 10 minutes by car via the A560 and Cheadle. From the Heaton Mersey/Heaton Moor side (SK4), it's similar — straight down the A5145 through Didsbury and into Gatley. From Edgeley and the SK3 postcode, slightly quicker. Free parking on site, so the drive ends at the chair, not at a meter.

Why Stockport Clients Drive

Two reasons usually. One: the cut. We've put sixteen years into the craft and it shows — particularly on the technical stuff like skin fades, beard sculpting, and the kind of mid-length scissor work most shops don't bother with. Two: the experience. BXRBERS is built for people who like the haircut to be a proper thirty minutes of their week, not a quick five-minute conveyor-belt job.

The Commute Angle

Plenty of Stockport clients work in Manchester city centre — Spinningfields, Ancoats, NQ — and finish too late to make a Stockport barber before close. BXRBERS' After Hours appointments run Wednesday to Saturday, 6:30 to 8:30pm. You can finish work, get on the train back to Stockport, and still get a proper cut the same evening. £35 for a cut, £50 for cut and beard. More on After Hours →

Live-event nights — Stockport County matchdays, gigs at The Plaza — book ahead. Evenings fill quickly.
