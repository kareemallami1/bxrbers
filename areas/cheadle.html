BXRBERS is in Gatley — about a mile from Cheadle high street, depending which end you're starting from. Plenty of our regulars are from Cheadle, SK8 1, SK8 2 and SK8 5 postcodes. The drive's nothing. The haircut's worth it.

The Journey

From Cheadle Village it's a four-minute drive down Gatley Road. From the Cheadle Royal side, slightly longer through Cheadle Hulme. From the SK8 5 postcode (towards Bruntwood Park), it's a six-minute drive. Free parking on site means you're not circling for ten minutes when you arrive — pull up, walk in.

Plenty of clients pair it with a coffee on Cheadle high street before or after — Foundation, Patisserie Valerie, the usual suspects. Make a thing of it.

Why Cheadle Clients Come

Mostly because the cut is better. We've heard the same story too many times — local Cheadle barber's fine for a quick tidy-up, but if you want a proper skin fade, a proper beard sculpt, or just someone who actually listens during the consultation, you end up coming to us. 5.0 stars across 463+ Booksy reviews didn't happen by accident.

The other reason: After Hours. Cheadle's full of professionals who can't make a daytime appointment, and most Cheadle barbershops close at 6. We do dedicated evening slots Wednesday to Saturday, 6:30–8:30pm. See After Hours →
