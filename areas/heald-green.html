Why Heald Green Comes To Us

Heald Green is full of professionals — airline crew, airport-adjacent workers, finance and tech commuters into Manchester. The schedules are weird. The shifts are long. A barbershop that closes at 6pm doesn't cut it. BXRBERS runs daytime appointments and dedicated After Hours slots Wednesday to Saturday, 6:30 to 8:30pm. See After Hours →

The other half: people who've been getting the same average cut from the same Heald Green barber for years and want something sharper. Skin fades, properly blended. Beard work that's actually shaped, not just trimmed. The standard you'd hope for at a proper barbershop and don't always get.

The Airport Angle

Plenty of our Heald Green regulars work shifts at Manchester Airport — five minutes the other direction. A pre-flight cut on the way to a long-haul is a thing more often than you'd expect. If you're crew, tell us when you book and we'll work around your shift pattern.

Same-day haircut before a flight? Call ahead — if a chair's free, walk in. Don't gamble on a Saturday morning.
