Six minutes by car. A shop the whole family ends up at.

Cheadle Hulme runs on family routines. School run, work, kids' clubs, weekend lunches. We see a lot of dads bringing in their kids, then booking themselves in for the next slot. £20 cuts, £15 for under-12s. 5.0 stars across 463+ Booksy reviews.

The Journey

From SK8 5 (the village side of Cheadle Hulme) it's six minutes via Cheadle Road. From SK8 6 (towards Bramhall Lane) seven. From SK8 7 (further out, towards Woodford), nine. Free parking on site so the school-run pickup doesn't end with you circling for ten minutes.

The Family Cut

We do plenty of family-stacked bookings — Dad in one chair, son in the next, on a Saturday morning. Book back-to-back slots on Booksy and you're in and out in an hour. The kids' cut is £15 and gets the same proper consultation as the adult one, just slightly shorter. See kids' haircuts →

If it's a first cut for the youngest, tell us when you book. We'll take it gently, keep it short, and won't push for perfection on the first visit. Plenty of Cheadle Hulme families have a "first cut" photo in our chair.

The After Hours Angle

You'll know this one. The day's gone, the kids are down, and you suddenly remember it's been six weeks since your last haircut. After Hours runs Wednesday to Saturday, 6:30 to 8:30pm — properly quiet, properly easy, no queue. £35 a cut. Most dads who try it once book it permanently. More on After Hours →

Sunday's closed, but Saturday daytime is the family-stacked slot. Book early in the week — Saturdays fill up by Wednesday.
