Skin Fade
done properly.

Sharp at the skin. Soft where it needs to be. Clean through every guard.

A skin fade isn't just a haircut — it's a test of the barber. The blend has to be invisible. The line has to be precise. The top has to actually go with the bottom. We've put the hours into all of it. £20 at BXRBERS Gatley, walk-ins welcome.

How We Fade

Low fade, mid fade, high fade, drop fade. Skin or taper. Burst around the ears. Whatever the brief is, the principle's the same: every guard blends into the next without a line you can see. That's the entire job.

Most fades you see online look sharp in the chair and fall apart a week later. Ours don't, because we cut with the grow-out in mind. You'll get two and a half good weeks out of a BXRBERS fade, not five days.

If You're New To Fades

You don't have to know what a "0.5 to a 2" means. You don't need a reference photo. Show us a vibe — short, sharp, neat at the back, a bit of length on top — and we'll work out the rest in the consultation. We do this every day. Nine times out of ten the first fade is the easiest haircut we'll do that week, because it's the kind of cut barbers actually like cutting.

If you've had a bad fade somewhere else, bring it in. We've fixed plenty. We won't make you feel weird about it.
