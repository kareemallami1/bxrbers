Kids' haircuts.
Properly done.

Patience. Banter. A proper cut, not a quick once-over.

Plenty of kids come into a barbershop and leave with a haircut that doesn't actually suit them, because nobody took the time. We take the time. £15 for under-12s at BXRBERS Gatley. Walk-ins welcome.

What To Expect

Same chair as everyone else. No special pretend-clipper toy, no booster seat that makes them feel like a baby. We treat kids like clients — we ask them what they want, we listen, and we cut. If they're nervous, we slow it down. If they're a regular, we already know how it goes.

Parents can sit in. Phones come out for the ones who want a distraction. The whole thing is usually done in 25 minutes and they leave looking like themselves, only sharper.

Cuts We Do A Lot Of

  • School-friendly tapers — short on the sides, length on top, easy to manage
  • Low and mid fades for the kids who want it to look like Dad's
  • Classic short back and sides — still the best cut for a school photo
  • Textured crops, curtains, and grow-outs done properly

For First-Timers

If it's their very first haircut, tell us when you book. We'll keep it short and gentle, take a photo of the curls if you want one, and not push for a perfect cut on day one. The second visit is always easier than the first.

Brothers booking together? Use the family slot on Booksy — book them back-to-back so you only park once.

Where We Cut

BXRBERS is on Silverdale Road in Gatley, with free parking on site. Families come in from Cheadle, Cheadle Hulme, Stockport, Heald Green, Bramhall, and Didsbury. We're easy to get to, easy to park at, and easy to leave from when school pickup is at three.
