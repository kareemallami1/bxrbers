After Hours.
For after work.

When five-thirty's never going to happen. Wednesday to Saturday, six-thirty to eight-thirty.

Most barbershops close at six. That's exactly when most people finish work. After Hours is BXRBERS' answer: dedicated evening slots, by appointment only, Wednesday through Saturday. £35 for a cut, £50 for cut and beard. No queues. No rush.

Why It Exists

Because there's a gap. Lads who finish work at 5:30 or 6:00, can't move meetings, can't take a half-day for a haircut. Lads with kids whose only spare hour is after bedtime. Shift workers. Commuters coming back into Manchester from Birmingham or London. They all need a haircut, and they all need it after the rest of the world closes.

After Hours is appointment-only and a different rhythm from the daytime shop. The lights are different, the music's different, it's quieter. The cut takes as long as it takes — there's no one queueing behind you.

How It Works

  • Book on Booksy, select the After Hours service
  • Choose a Wednesday, Thursday, Friday or Saturday slot between 6:30pm and 8:30pm
  • Turn up, sit down, get cut. Same chair, same standard as the daytime cut.
  • Cut: £35 · Cut + Beard: £50

Worth The Premium?

Honestly — only you can decide. The premium pays for the late slot, the appointment-only quiet, and the fact you're not sitting in a waiting area on a Saturday afternoon. For people who can't make daytime work, it's the difference between a proper cut and going another fortnight scruffy. For people who can — daytime's still £20.

Booking opens in the same Booksy widget as daytime appointments. Just filter for evening times — you'll see them flagged as After Hours.

Who Uses It

People who commute into Manchester city centre and don't get back until 7. Dads who can finally get out once the kids are down. Anyone who's tired of trying to schedule a haircut around a job and just wants the appointment to be there when they need it. We've had clients drive in from Wilmslow, Hale, Altrincham, Sale, and across South Manchester for the After Hours slot.
