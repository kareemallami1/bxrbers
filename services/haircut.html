The Cut

You sit down. We talk about what you want. Not from a chart, not from a script — properly. We agree what's going to work, and then we cut. Skin fade, taper, scissor crop, side part, French crop, textured top, classic short back and sides — every cut gets the same attention to detail. If you don't know what to ask for, that's fine. We'll work it out together.

Every haircut at BXRBERS includes the consultation, the cut, a clean line-up, eyebrows if you want them, and a finish you can actually replicate at home. We use what works — clippers, scissors, razor, comb — not whatever's trendy that month.

What's Included

  • Consultation — proper one, not a quick "what are we doing?"
  • Wash if you want one
  • The cut, however long it takes
  • Line-up at the neck and around the ears
  • Eyebrow tidy on request
  • Style and finish

Who We Cut

Students. Professionals. Lads who've been getting the same cut for ten years. Lads who've never had a fade and want to try one. Dads bringing in their kids. Anyone who wants their hair done properly, by someone who actually cares whether they get it right.

BXRBERS is in Gatley but we cut for clients from across South Manchester — Cheadle, Cheadle Hulme, Stockport, Heald Green, Bramhall, Didsbury, and beyond. Free parking on site means it's actually worth the drive.

The Standard

Same chair, same standard, every time. That's not a policy — it's just how we work. Whether you're in for a £20 cut or a £50 evening cut-and-beard, you get the same attention. The craft is taken seriously. And so are you.
