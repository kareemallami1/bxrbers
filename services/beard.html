Beard work,

Shaping. Edging. A proper finish. Not just a tidy-up.

A beard is half your face. It deserves the same attention as the cut on top. We do beard work as a standalone service for £15, or paired with a haircut for £30 total. Sixteen years of practice. Walk-ins welcome.

What Counts As Beard Work

Most "beard trims" are a quick run-over with the clippers. That's not what we do. A proper beard at BXRBERS means actually shaping the beard to the face — the cheek line, the neck line, the way it sits against the sideburns and into the fade if you've got one. Then the detail work: scissor finish on the longer hair, razor for the edges, a tight line where it needs to be tight.

If you want it grown out and natural, we'll work with that. If you want it short and architectural, we'll work with that too. The brief is yours.

What's Included

  • Consultation — beard shape, length, density, how you want it to sit
  • Clipper work to length
  • Cheek line and neck line cut clean with a razor
  • Scissor finish on the longer beard hair where needed
  • Hot towel where appropriate
  • A finish you can keep going at home

Beards We Cut Most Often

The short stubble shape, kept sharp every two weeks. The fuller medium beard with a defined cheek line. The long, full beard that just needs the edges and the neck kept clean. The beard fade that runs from the side hair into the jaw without a visible step. We've done all of them. Bring a picture if it helps, or just talk us through what you're after.

If your beard's patchy and you're not sure what's possible, ask. Most patchy beards look better shaped down a level — we'll tell you straight.

Hot Towel & Finish

Where it makes sense, the beard work finishes with a hot towel — softens the skin, lifts what's left of the stubble, and feels like the end of something. Aftershave balm or beard oil to finish. You'll leave with a beard you can actually keep.
